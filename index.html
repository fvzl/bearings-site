Common questions

How does the heading work?

Each habit has a heading: the share of days you want to show up over a recent window, 85 percent over four weeks by default. The landscape photo shows how you're doing against it. You can change a habit's heading when you edit it, and how far back Bearings looks in Settings.

My habits aren't syncing between devices.

  • Make sure every device is signed into iCloud with the same Apple ID.
  • Open Bearings on each device and give it a minute; the very first sync can take a little longer.
  • In the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and check that Bearings is allowed to use iCloud.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Bearings. Check Off lets you log a habit with one tap; the Beacon shows your overall status. The Beacon also comes in Lock Screen sizes, and both work in StandBy.

How do I use Bearings on Apple Watch?

The watch app installs with the iPhone app. If it doesn't appear, open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps, and tap Install next to Bearings. Complications are in the watch face editor.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Settings → Export Data creates a file you can keep or move to another device, and Import Data reads it back.

What do tips unlock?

Nothing. Everything in Bearings is free. Tips are optional thanks to the developer.
