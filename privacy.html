Bearings

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026.

The short version

Bearings collects nothing. Your habits stay yours.

What Bearings stores

Everything you enter — habits, completions, reminders, notes to your future self — is stored on your device. If your device is signed into iCloud, the same data syncs through your private iCloud database, which only your Apple ID can access. We (the developer) cannot read, see, or receive any of it.

What Bearings does NOT do

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind. The app contains no analytics code and no third-party software.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers.
  • No data is ever transmitted to the developer or to anyone else.

Purchases

In-app purchases (such as optional tips) are processed entirely by Apple. We receive Apple's standard sales reports, which contain no personal information.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Notification content never leaves it.

Data export & deletion

You can export your data anytime from Settings → Export Data. Deleting the app deletes all local data; iCloud copies can be removed by deleting the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

Bearings collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
